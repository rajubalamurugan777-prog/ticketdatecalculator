Welcome to trainticketcalc.in, a free online utility tool designed to help Indian Railway passengers calculate their Advance Reservation Period (ARP) booking opening dates accurately.

Planning train journeys during peak festival seasons and holidays can be challenging. Our objective is to make ticket planning simple, fast, and accessible for everyone by providing precise booking schedules based on official IRCTC reservation rules.
